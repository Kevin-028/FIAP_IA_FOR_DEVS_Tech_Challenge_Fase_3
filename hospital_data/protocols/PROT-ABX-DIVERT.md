# Antibiotics in uncomplicated diverticulitis

- protocol_id: PROT-ABX-DIVERT
- PMID: 22290281

Hospital protocol PROT-ABX-DIVERT. Antibiotics may not improve recovery in CT-confirmed uncomplicated diverticulitis (PMID 22290281). Suggested conduct: confirm imaging, pain control, oral intake, return precautions. REQUIRES PHYSICIAN VALIDATION.
