# Low-dose aspirin for preeclampsia prevention

- protocol_id: PROT-ASPIRIN-PREECL
- PMID: 25098060

Hospital protocol PROT-ASPIRIN-PREECL. Low-dose aspirin started in the second trimester reduces preeclampsia in high-risk pregnancy (PMID 25098060). Suggested conduct: confirm risk factors in the chart, obstetric review. REQUIRES PHYSICIAN VALIDATION. Never prescribe from this assistant.
