# PPI before endoscopy for upper GI bleeding

- protocol_id: PROT-PPI-UGIB
- PMID: 18973770

Hospital protocol PROT-PPI-UGIB. High-dose proton pump inhibitor before endoscopy may reduce the need for endoscopic therapy in upper GI bleeding (PMID 18973770, evidence mixed — label maybe when trials conflict). Suggested conduct: ensure IV access, type and screen, urgent endoscopy pathway. REQUIRES PHYSICIAN VALIDATION. Do not start a drug from this assistant.
