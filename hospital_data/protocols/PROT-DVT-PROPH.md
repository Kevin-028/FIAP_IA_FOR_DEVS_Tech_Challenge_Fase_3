# VTE prophylaxis in medical inpatients

- protocol_id: PROT-DVT-PROPH
- PMID: 16549822

Hospital protocol PROT-DVT-PROPH. Pharmacologic VTE prophylaxis reduces events in at-risk medical inpatients (PMID 16549822) unless bleeding risk is high. Suggested conduct: review bleeding risk and mobility in the chart. REQUIRES PHYSICIAN VALIDATION. Do not prescribe heparin here.
