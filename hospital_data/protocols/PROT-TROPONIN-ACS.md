# Serial troponin in suspected ACS

- protocol_id: PROT-TROPONIN-ACS
- PMID: 25173350

Hospital protocol PROT-TROPONIN-ACS. A single negative troponin does not exclude ACS. Repeat high-sensitivity troponin per pathway (PMID 25173350). Suggested conduct: if chest pain and first troponin pending or negative, keep the patient monitored and emit an alert for the pending exam. REQUIRES PHYSICIAN VALIDATION.
